How a plugin gets to your club

We review, you switch it on.

  1. Reviewed and installed

    A plugin runs with access to the platform's data. So only Hubra's platform admins install plugins, after a review, in the plugin catalogue.

  2. Your board switches it on

    Under Settings › Modules & plugins you switch a plugin on or off for your club, like any built-in module.

  3. Signed updates

    Every version is signed and comes with its changes. A Hubra installs only what is signed with a key it trusts, and can go back to the previous version.
